Threat Level: Alpha // Level 1 Guardian // Bio-Weapon

Subject 004, codenamed "Tanker," is a massive biological anomaly encountered in the primary containment halls. Resulting from an overdose of experimental growth serums, this subject has developed impenetrable muscle density at the cost of its higher brain functions. It serves as a living wall for the facility.
